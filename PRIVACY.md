# Kinwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

## Operator and contact
Kinwell is a family wellness prototype operated by Matthew Christensen. Contact mattchristensen503@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

This notice covers the current local prototype. Oura and WHOOP connections are under development and are not enabled. This notice will be updated before wearable data collection or public application hosting begins.

## Information collected and purposes
Kinwell stores your name, email address, salted password hash, family membership, sharing preference, invitation records, and session information. It also stores sleep dates and durations you voluntarily enter. These records support accounts, family membership, sign-in, personal summaries and your selected sharing settings. The server processes connection information, including IP addresses, for request handling and temporary login throttling.

Kinwell supports personal wellness tracking. It does not diagnose conditions or determine who is medically healthiest.

## Family sharing and administrator access
Your name and sharing status are visible to your family members. Sleep sharing is off by default. If enabled, your family can view your sleep entries included in the rolling seven-day family dashboard and associated summaries. Turning sharing off prevents subsequent family access through that dashboard, but cannot remove copies or screenshots others already made.

The server administrator can technically access stored information for administration and support. Family privacy controls do not prevent that administrative access.

## Storage and security
The current application stores records in a database on the operator's computer. Passwords are stored as salted hashes; session cookies support sign-in and expire after seven days. Expired session records are removed during subsequent successful sign-ins. The application does not currently encrypt the database itself. Protection also depends on computer access controls and device security. No system guarantees absolute security.

The application is currently local-only. Hosting providers, transport security and backup arrangements will be disclosed and configured before public health-data collection begins.

## Cookies, service providers and disclosure
Kinwell uses a necessary session cookie. The current application does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics, sell personal information, or send wellness entries to AI services.

This public policy is hosted on GitHub. GitHub processes requests to this page under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement . The public policy repository does not contain Kinwell accounts, health records, passwords or API credentials.

## Choices, retention and deletion
You can replace or delete manual sleep entries and turn family sharing on or off. Other account information remains stored until administratively removed. The prototype has no automatic account-data retention schedule or self-service account deletion. Contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion. The dashboard's display limits do not delete older stored entries. Backup retention and deletion procedures will be described before backups are introduced.

## Planned wearable integrations
Kinwell currently imports no Oura or WHOOP information. Before enabling these integrations, this policy will describe the specific data collected, how authorization and disconnect work, token storage, retention, deletion and any additional providers. Registering a developer application alone does not connect your account or import your wearable data.

## Changes
The effective date will change when this policy is revised. Affected users will be informed of material changes before new uses or sharing are introduced. For questions, contact mattchristensen503@gmail.com.
